Redesign 4 · Miro Float
Miro's objects float over a board feel, applied without giving up the list. Every item is a white card with a soft shadow, so rows read as movable things — which they are, since drag-reorder already exists but the flat list gives no hint of it (hence the visible grip). Sections move to a floating left dock, always visible with counts, freeing the full width for the title. Composer floats as a pill, long-press raises a card and shows a contextual toolbar including move-to-section. Yellow FAB carries the primary add.

Cost: cheapest of the four to build — same LazyColumn, same reorder library, just new row chrome and a Box-anchored dock. Card padding costs ~2 visible rows versus today's flat list. Yellow is a second accent competing with Section.color; drop it for the section accent if that bothers you.

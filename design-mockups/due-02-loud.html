Due 2 · Loud — same data as Due 1, four extra signals: an amber left bar and wash, text at 600, a relative-time chip (Due today / 3d late), and the glyph on a tinted disc. Undue rows gain their own quiet chip (in 12d) so the wait is legible without opening a tooltip, and the divider becomes a labelled band carrying a count.

Amber, not the section colour. Due-ness has to mean the same thing in every section, and section colours are already nine saturated accents — reusing them would make "due" read as "Shopping". The cost is a tenth fixed colour in an app whose only other fixed colours are the rail and the badge.

The header and badge also change: 3 open · 2 due, and the rail badge flips amber when the section holds something due. That's the part Due 1 can't do — it makes a due task visible from a section you aren't looking at. It also means observeOpenCounts has to return a second number per section, not just a count.

Built without the chips. "Due today" / "3d late" / "in 12d" each cost width on a row that already carries a glyph and a ⋮, and the tooltip says the same thing on demand — so the bar, wash, weight and amber badge shipped, and every chip drawn above did not. Relative time survives only in the tooltip title, where being a day stale past midnight doesn't matter.

Migration and count costs are identical to Due 1 — dueAt + repeatWeeks nullable at 6 → 7, both ordering queries changed together, both "open" definitions edited. Nothing here is cheaper or dearer at the data layer; the whole difference is pixels.

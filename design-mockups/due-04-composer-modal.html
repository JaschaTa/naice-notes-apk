Composer button — three states

Third control in the tool rail, beside scan and the Claude checkbox. Unlike the Claude checkbox — which is deliberately sticky so a run of captures doesn't need re-arming — the schedule clears on submit, because a due date belongs to one task, not to a mood you're in.

Due 4 · Composer button & modal. One modal covers both features: a Due row of quick chips for the common intervals plus an escape hatch to a real date picker, and a Repeat switch that reveals a weeks stepper. A one-off is a due chip with repeat off; a recurring task is both. Repeat on with no due chip is owed straight away — the cycle starts from the first reset, not from setting it up.

Chips before calendar. "In two weeks" is the actual thought — going through a month grid to express it is three taps and a date you then have to remember. The calendar stays for the cases chips can't say ("the 14th, when the warranty ends"). Material3 ships DatePickerDialog, so the escape hatch is free.

Weeks only in the stepper. Matches the ask and keeps repeatWeeks INTEGER a plain count. Days and months would each need a unit enum in the schema — worth it only if the need shows up.

Unresolved: schedule + Claude together. A note sent to Claude leaves the app and is never read back, so a due date on it is meaningless — the row it would attach to doesn't stay. Cleanest is mutually exclusive: arming one clears the other, which is one line at each call site but needs to not feel like the app is undoing your taps. Shown here with Claude unchecked, deliberately.

Reset-on-submit has a trap. text and the Claude checkbox are rememberSaveable in Composer; the schedule has to live in the same place and be cleared in submit() alongside text. Miss that and the next three tasks silently inherit a due date — the kind of bug you don't notice for two weeks, by which time the evidence has scrolled below a divider.
